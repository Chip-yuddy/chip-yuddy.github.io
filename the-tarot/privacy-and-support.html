Privacy Policy

The Tarot by Niteshift Studio · Last updated 17 November 2026

The Tarot is a tarot reading app with no accounts and no sign-up. It works fully offline, and it only uses the internet if you choose a reading written by Claude, an AI from Anthropic. This page explains exactly what happens to information when you use it.

Stays on your phone

Your saved journal, your choices in Privacy & AI, and the built-in offline reader. The offline reader never sends anything anywhere. If you tap Share Reading, the app draws an image of your card on your own phone and hands it to Apple's share sheet — we never see or receive it, and it's only created when you choose to share. Deleting the app removes everything stored on your phone.

Readings written by Claude (optional)

A reading written by Claude is only used if you allow it, on the consent screen that appears before the first one. If you allow it, the app sends the following to our server, which passes it to Anthropic, the company that makes Claude, so that it can write the reading:

  • the question you typed (if you typed one)
  • the cards drawn and their positions
  • today's date and the astrology and numerology used in the reading

Please don't put private or personal details in your question. We do not save your question or the reading on our server, and our server does not log them. Anthropic handles the information under its own terms and privacy policy. You can turn Claude readings off at any time in Privacy & AI, and the offline reader keeps working.

Kept on our server

To work out who gets a free reading, and to stop abuse, our server (hosted by Supabase) keeps:

  • a random anonymous ID made by the app. It is not linked to your name, Apple ID or any account
  • how many free readings the app has used today, and any reading credits earned from watching ads
  • a scrambled (hashed) form of your network address, which can't be turned back into the address. It is used only to limit abuse and is deleted after 7 days

Short-lived records of recent readings, kept only so a failed reading can be refunded, are deleted after 2 days.

Adverts

The Tarot is free and is paid for by adverts from Google AdMob. A small banner advert is shown along the bottom of the screen, and you can optionally watch a short rewarded video advert to earn another Claude reading. Shortly after the app first opens, it may ask for:

  • Google's consent message (in the UK, the EU and some other places), and
  • Apple's tracking permission. If you allow it, Google may use your device's advertising identifier to choose adverts. If you decline, adverts still show but are not personalised.

To show adverts and count them fairly, Google's software collects information such as your IP address (to estimate rough location), device and app information, which adverts were shown, and diagnostic data. When you finish a rewarded video, Google confirms it to our server using the anonymous ID above, so we can add the reading credit. Google handles this under its own privacy policy; see also how Google uses information from apps that use its services. Declining tracking or personalised adverts never stops you getting readings: the offline reader is always free.

What we don't do

  • We don't ask you to sign up or log in.
  • We don't sell your information.
  • We don't use analytics or crash-reporting tools of our own.
  • Apart from Anthropic (Claude readings), Google (adverts) and Supabase (our server host), we don't share information with anyone.
  • We don't access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location.
  • We don't see or store the images created by the Share Reading feature — they're made on your phone and handed straight to Apple's own share sheet.

Your choices and rights

  • Allow or turn off Claude readings in Privacy & AI.
  • Decline tracking in Apple's prompt, or change it later in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Delete the app to remove everything stored on your phone.
  • Email us to ask about the server-side record. Because it is tied only to a random ID and not to your name, we may not be able to tell which record is yours, but we'll help where we can. These records are small and are removed or become inactive on their own.

If you're in the UK or EU, you also have rights under data protection law, including access and deletion. Contact us and we'll help.

Not advice

Readings are for reflection and entertainment. They are not medical, legal or financial advice.

Children

The Tarot is not aimed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

The Tarot Support

A Niteshift Studio app

Ask a question, shuffle and draw from a full 78-card deck with six spreads, from a single Daily Card to the Celtic Cross and a Year Ahead. Readings weave together your cards, today's Sun sign and moon phase, and the numerology of the day.

How it works

  • Offline reader: always free, works without internet, and sends nothing anywhere.
  • Claude readings: optional. The first each time you open the app is free, and you can watch a short ad for more.
  • Journal: tap Save Reading to keep a reading on your phone.
  • Sharing: tap Share Reading for an image of your card to post or send, with your choice of where it goes handled by your phone's own share sheet.

Something not working?

  • Claude reading didn't appear: check your internet connection. If it fails, you get the offline reading instead and you aren't charged a reading.
  • No advert available: adverts aren't always available. Try again later; the offline reader is always there.
  • The banner at the bottom: it's how the app stays free. Adverts are chosen by Google; you can turn off personalised adverts in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Lost my journal: it is kept on your phone only, so deleting the app removes it.
  • Changed your mind about Claude readings: open Privacy & AI at the bottom of any screen.
